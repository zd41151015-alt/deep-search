# Concept Evidence Assessment

`assess` creates a `concept_evidence_assessment` Run for one concrete product or feature thesis. This vertical slice is owned by G1 and is not operational in G0.1.

Every research branch must point to the same concept hypothesis. Required desk-research dimensions cover target user and JTBD, demand and behavior, alternatives and solution failure, competitor saturation and differentiation, buyer language and willingness to pay, acquisition and distribution, business engine viability, delivery feasibility, compliance/platform risk, and counter-evidence. AI profiles add their mandatory capability bundle.

The only assessment results are `prioritize`, `investigate_further`, `deprioritize`, and `insufficient_evidence`. Deterministic gates enforce mandatory coverage, evidence conclusion ceilings, and hard failures before an agent explains the result. Missing external behavior, commitment, or transaction evidence limits confidence; it does not automatically count as opposition.

This mode does not generate a TopN opportunity pool and does not execute or track interviews, landing pages, deposits, paid experiments, or MVP tests.
